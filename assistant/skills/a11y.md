## Rules

- Button labels say where they go and match their action: shop "See our products", visit "Visit us", call "Call
  us", email "Email us", directions "Get directions". Never "Click here", "More", "→" or a label that hides the
  action.
- Each block's heading says what the block is about in a few plain words; no heading written all in capital
  letters, and only the Hero holds the page's main headline. The editor has no heading-level setting: change the
  wording only.
- Text must be readable against its background (WCAG AA, 4.5:1 for normal text). Fix contrast only through the
  page's colour settings: set a text, background or accent colour override back to null (the palette's own
  colours are readable), or pick another palette. Never invent colours outside the palette.
- With "@" blocks, change only those blocks; mention a page-wide colour problem in the reply instead of fixing it,
  unless the owner asks.
- The editor has no picture descriptions (alt text): never write them into captions or headings. Say in the reply
  which pictures would need one.
- Plain words and short sentences help everyone: never leave text that is only symbols or emoji, and spell out an
  abbreviation only when the page or message says what it means (otherwise ask in the reply).
- Never touch props the assistant may not write (customer quotes, Stats numbers, products, images).
